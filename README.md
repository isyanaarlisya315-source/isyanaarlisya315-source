## About

I’m **Isyana Arlisya**, also known as **Musyi** — a playful twist on *musisi* and my pen name.

I am an aspiring Psychology undergraduate interested in the relationship between **music, emotion, and well-being**. I enjoy research, history, singing, writing, coding, and trying to understand people a little more gently.

My work is shaped by curiosity about how people feel, remember, express themselves, and find meaning in everyday life.
